Skill drills

3D Challenges

Short, scoped briefs pulled straight from the Lab. Pick a level, read the brief, build it, compare it against the studio bar.

01

Hero Prop Turntable

Beginner
Lighting Rendering

Light and render a single hero product as a clean 360° turntable. Three-point lighting minimum, one HDRI environment, 4-second loop. Judged on shading accuracy and consistency across the full rotation.

02

Material Study: Metal vs Glass

Beginner
Materials Shading

Build two matching props side by side — one brushed metal, one frosted glass — under identical lighting. The goal is to sell the material difference through shading alone, no textures required.

03

Cinematic Product Reveal

Intermediate
Camera Motion Lighting

A 6–10 second camera move that reveals a product from darkness into full light. Focus on pacing and the choreography between the camera and the lighting change — the product should feel like the hero of the shot.

04

Procedural Environment Blockout

Intermediate
Environment Procedural

Block out a small environment (a room or a street corner) using procedural or modular pieces only — no hand-sculpted hero assets. Judged on read and composition from a single fixed camera angle.

05

Character Lighting Pass

Advanced
Lighting Character

Given a pre-built character model, deliver a full lighting pass across three moods: neutral, dramatic, and warm/inviting. Same camera angle for all three — the lighting alone should carry the emotional shift.

06

Real-Time Showreel Cut

Advanced
Editing Motion Sound

Cut a 15–20 second showreel from raw real-time renders (yours or provided). Pacing, music sync and a strong final frame are the bar — this challenge is entirely about the edit, not the render.
